# ARC — Rebuild Decision Addendum

Status: CURRENT / GOVERNING

Date: September 8, 2026

This addendum records Kelly's current explicit direction and overrides conflicting implementation or asset instructions in older documents.

## Decision

1. Arc will be rebuilt through a new code-native interface rather than continued as a Figma- or Canva-dependent design process.
2. The existing implementation, documents, and assets remain recoverable as reference. They are not deleted.
3. The work is a faithful re-render of Arc's established visual integrity and product intent, not a new art direction.
4. Minor asset adjustments are permitted when needed for responsive web behavior, accessibility, optimization, contrast, tiling, antialiasing, or clean connection to live interface regions.
5. Existing uncertain Canva-derived material must not ship as a production dependency.
6. The full functional asset family is recreated in this package as original SVG masters and PNG renders.
7. Figma is no longer required as the implementation handoff or production asset source.
8. The package must be sufficient for Claude or a professional web design/development agency to understand, estimate, build, test, and hand off the product without relying on prior chat history.

## Unchanged product authority

The calendar remains the center. One canonical state powers all views. The teacher remains the authority. Nothing important disappears silently. Recovery, history, persistence, accessibility, and fixed calendar geometry remain non-negotiable.

## Asset authority for the rebuild

For the new implementation, `asset-manifest.csv`, `asset-metadata.json`, `tokens.css`, and the files under `assets/svg` and `assets/png` are the portable replacement asset family. Historical asset references remain evidence of role and prior appearance only.

## Conflict rule

If an older document requires a Figma node, Canva export, Adobe share link, or specific historical vector as the production master, this addendum replaces that requirement. Preserve the intended role, palette, spatial relationship, and visual character using the supplied replacement asset or a minimally adjusted derivative with recorded provenance.
