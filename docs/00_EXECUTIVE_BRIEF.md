# ARC — Executive Brief

## Purpose

Arc is a calendar-centered teaching workspace that helps a teacher know where they are, what changed, and what happens next. Its shortest product test is: **Arc holds your place.**

Arc connects planning, live teaching, disruption, recovery, and later reuse without pretending to be the teaching expert. It supports the teacher's existing Drive, Canva, LMS, calendar, presentation, and resource links rather than trying to replace them.

## Audience

The primary user is an experienced classroom teacher working under ordinary school disruption: assemblies, testing, absences, weather, partial lessons, pacing differences, early release, and multiple Sections of the same Course. The product must remain understandable when the teacher is tired and pressed for time.

## Product center

The calendar is Home. Day, Week, Month, Quarter, Semester, and Year Map are lenses over one canonical teaching state, not independent schedule stores. The current build priority is trustworthy continuity across Day, Week, and Month before expanding every horizon.

## Required product loop

Capture → sort → place → teach → adjust → recover → reuse.

## Agency mandate

Re-render Arc faithfully as a responsive web application. Preserve the established planner-centered spatial model, edge furniture, tactile editorial tone, and canonical object distinctions. Implement the behavior on a clean reactive-state foundation. Do not copy old component structure blindly, and do not redesign Arc into a sidebar/dashboard/card grid.

## Non-negotiables

- One canonical state across every calendar view.
- Stable object identity and reload-safe history/undo.
- No silent data loss or movement.
- Fixed dates remain fixed unless explicitly changed.
- The teacher approves consequential automation.
- Fridge, Settings, and Task Bar are exterior-edge furniture; they do not cover, squeeze, or reflow the calendar.
- Day is the only launch point for Live Classroom.
- Dragging is optional enhancement; click, keyboard, and touch alternatives are equally capable.
- Accessibility and recovery are release requirements.
- No production dependency on Canva or Figma artwork.

## What success feels like

High-end grown-up stationery and editorial utility: calm, tactile, contemporary, slightly imperfect. About 80% structure and 20% expression. A capable colleague, not corporate ed-tech and not teacher-cute.
